# Monochrome launcher marks

Brand marks identify their respective services in Home. They are not endorsements. White paths are recoloured from the linked SVGs; size and opacity are controlled by the interface. Labels disambiguate the streaming services. No fabricated monograms.

Simple Icons sources are CC0; trademarks remain with their owners. Custom Brand Icons retains its MIT licence; this source record does not grant trademark rights. See SOURCES.json for exact revisions and adaptations.

Prime Video uses its complete wordmark (`primevideo-wordmark.svg`). Apple Music uses the note subpath without the outer app tile. These are intentional simplified marks beside full service names. Simple Icons CC0 and Custom Brand Icons MIT texts are included here. The old Australian TV source GPL text is retained for provenance only; no current icon uses that source.
