## Fonts and icons

Public fonts are Manrope, Geist and Newsreader under SIL OFL 1.1. Their licence texts are beside the assets; Geist and Newsreader source URLs and hashes are in `app/assets/fonts/SOURCES.json`.

Icon sources, exact revisions and adaptation notes are in `app/assets/icons/SOURCES.json` and `ADDITIONAL-SOURCES.json`. Disney+, Hulu, PBS, Peacock and Pluto TV use the MIT Custom Brand Icons project; other service icons use Simple Icons under CC0. See [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for the retained licence texts and trademark scope.
